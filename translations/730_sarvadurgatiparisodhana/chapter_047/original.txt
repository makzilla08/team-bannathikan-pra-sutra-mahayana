Section 47 of 89
============================================================

m idaṃ kuru tat kartavyaṃ na ca tvayāham avamantavyo mā te viṣamāparihāreṇa kālakriyāṃ kṛtvā narake patanaṃ syād iti|
( 46 )
tad anu akāraṃ vajraraśmimālāyuktaṃ svahṛdi cintayet| tataḥ śiṣyahṛdūrṇākaṇṭhamūrdhniṣu candramaṇḍalasthapañcasūcikaṃ jvālāvajraṃ ratnaṃ padmaṃ viśvavajraṃ ca cintayet| ebhir yathākrameṇa huṃ trāṃ hṛḥ aḥ iti|

ayan tat samayavajraṃ vajrasattva iti smṛtam||
